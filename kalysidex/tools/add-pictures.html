Add pictures to a dex

Drop in a folder of pictures and this page matches them to the Pokémon in a dex, resizes them, and makes a zip you can unzip into your Kalysidex folder. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Extract your pictures into one folder (all the images together, subfolders are fine).
  2. Pick the folder below and choose the dex. Preview matches shows which picture went with which Pokémon.
  3. Fix any mismatches by clicking the buttons under a tile, press Make zip, then Download zip, and unzip it over your Kalysidex folder (choose Replace if asked).

Pictures are matched either by file name (bulbasaur.png, or Deoxys Attack Forme.png) or by order (the 1st picture goes with the 1st Pokémon in the dex, and so on, in file-name order). Order is what you want for pictures taken out of a PDF in page order.
